# Mongolia competitive landscape for a consumer personal finance / budgeting app (as of 25 Nov 2026)

> **Method and reliability note (read first).** In this session only web *search* worked; direct page fetches were blocked by the network allowlist (apps.apple.com, datareportal.com, frc.mn, mongolbank.mn, gogo.mn, coda.co, mongoliafintech.org all failed to resolve). Every finding below therefore comes from search-result summaries/snippets of the linked page, not from reading the full page. Treat exact figures as "reported in snippet" and re-verify on the primary page before publishing. Where a snippet did not make clear which of several returned URLs a number came from, that is flagged "(URL attribution uncertain)". Nothing here is invented; anything not found is listed under Gaps.

## 1. Who are the direct competitors (Mongolian-made budgeting / income-expense tracker apps, and global apps used in Mongolia)?

### Takeaway
The direct-competitor field is thin and weak: a handful of small Mongolian-language manual trackers (MyFin, Savely, Savvyx, Finexy, the Bank of Mongolia-linked "Өрхийн төсөв" e-notebook) with low rating counts and no evidence of scale, bank linking, or a proven paid tier. None of the apps named in the brief (MoneyM, "Мөнгөн урсгал", "Зарлага", "Хэтэвч", Sainscore) could be confirmed to exist as a budgeting product in search results.

### Cited Findings
- **MyFin – Орлого Зарлага** (iOS id1563540663): Mongolian-language app; lets the user keep household and personal finances in parallel; data stored in the cloud so it survives loss of the phone; also pitched at small-business owners. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/qa/app/myfin-%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE-%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B3%D0%B0/id1563540663)
- **Savely: Орлого, зардал бүртгэл** (iOS id6444849010): register bank accounts and cash manually, track income/expense by category, charts by week/month/year; App Store rating 3.5/5 from 44 ratings (snapshot seen in search, date unknown). — [App Store MN listing](https://apps.apple.com/mn/app/savely/id6444849010)
- Savely's developer is listed as **Bitsoft LLC**, price "free" on the US store, Finance category, outside the top 30; it describes itself as an all-in-one budget and expense tracker with category progress circles and also savings and crypto tracking. No Premium tier/price was visible in the aggregator snapshot. — [mwm.ai aggregator page](https://mwm.ai/apps/savely/6444849010)
- Caution: several unrelated foreign apps share the name "Savely" (e.g. "Money Tracker: Savely Budget" by Salah-eddine KABLY with EUR in-app purchases; comsavely.com priced in BRL). Their prices are **not** the Mongolian app's. — [App Store IE listing](https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/money-tracker-savely-budget/id6760823221); [comsavely.com](https://comsavely.com/)
- **Savvyx – Орлого зарлага бүртгэл** (iOS id6759860780): quick expense entry, charts and reports of spending patterns, simple UI. The App Store ID range (67598…) indicates a 2026 release (inference from ID, not a stated date). — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759860780)
- **Finexy** (iOS id6759546900): for individuals and families; categorised income/expense, savings goals, and a "family mode" for members to record together. Found via a Mexican-storefront page, so Mongolian origin/language support is unverified. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/finexy/id6759546900)
- **Bank of Mongolia financial-literacy tools**: sankhuugiinbolovsrol.mn has a "Тооцоолуур" (Tools) section where a user records monthly/regular income and expenses to see where money goes, and enters loan details to track repayments and end date. — [sankhuugiinbolovsrol.mn/Tools](https://www.sankhuugiinbolovsrol.mn/Tools)
- A Bank of Mongolia household-budget handbook points readers to an electronic household-budget notebook and says its apps are on Google Play and the App Store. — [mongolbank.mn handbook "Өрхийн төсөв" (35.pdf)](https://www.mongolbank.mn/file/files/documents/financialliteracy/publications/35.pdf)
- **urkhiintusuv.mn ("Өрхийн төсөв програм")**: presented as an electronic notebook for recording household income/expenses and planning a budget; whether it is the official Bank of Mongolia product was not confirmed. — [urkhiintusuv.mn](http://urkhiintusuv.mn/)
- A Mongolian open-source hobby project "bayan app" appeared for the personal-finance query (GitHub), i.e. developer-side interest exists but no commercial product was evidenced. — [GitHub khembile/bayan-app](https://github.com/khembile/bayan-app)
- **Global apps**: Realbyte Money Manager's two help-centre language lists do not show Mongolian (lists seen in snippets may be incomplete). — [Realbyte help 1](https://help.realbyteapps.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047247933-How-to-change-the-language); [Realbyte help 2](https://help.realbyteapps.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042988134-How-to-change-the-language)
- Spendee advertises bank sync, but nothing in results indicates Mongolian banks or Mongolian language are supported. — [spendee.com](https://www.spendee.com/)
- MNT is a standard ISO 4217 currency code, so global trackers that offer full ISO currency lists can generally be set to tugrik (app-by-app confirmation not found). — [Localizely MNT](https://localizely.com/currency-code/MNT/)

### Inferences
- No direct competitor shows evidence of meaningful scale (the only rating count found is 44 for Savely); the category is effectively unowned in Mongolia.
- All local trackers found are manual-entry; none advertises bank-statement import, SMS/notification parsing, e-Barimt import, or loan/BNPL aggregation. Those are open differentiation axes.
- The newest entrants (Savvyx, Finexy, 2026-range App Store IDs) suggest fresh, small indie entries in 2026, i.e. the idea is "in the air" but nobody is established.
- Global apps compete only on feature depth; lack of Mongolian UI and of any Mongolian bank connectivity is their structural weakness.

### Gaps
- **MoneyM, "Мөнгөн урсгал", "Зарлага", "Хэтэвч"**: no search result confirmed a Mongolian budgeting app with these names. Could not be verified to exist.
- **Sainscore / "Сайн скор"**: nothing found; cannot say whether it is a credit-score or budgeting product, or its users.
- Google Play listings/installs for MyFin, Savely, Savvyx, Finexy: not surfaced by search; install bands unknown.
- Pricing/IAP, review texts, last-update dates and user counts for all direct competitors: not retrievable (App Store pages could not be fetched).
- Money Lover and Wallet by BudgetBakers: no evidence found either way on Mongolian language; MNT support unconfirmed.
- Popularity ranking of global trackers in the Mongolian App Store/Play finance charts: not found.

## 2. Who are the indirect competitors (bank apps, wallets, super-apps, BNPL/lending apps, e-Barimt) and how big are they?

### Takeaway
Scale sits with banks and wallets: Khan Bank (about 1.8M digital users at end-2023, 1M+ Play installs), MonPay (reported 2.1M users), Toki (1M+ registered, 150k+ MAU), Storepay (600k+ users, dated), and the e-Barimt system (about 1.4M citizens receive quarterly VAT refunds). No evidence was found that any of them markets a real budgeting/PFM feature set; that absence is unverified rather than proven.

### Cited Findings
- **Khan Bank**: digital platform had 1.8 million users at end-2023, described as 55% of the population; about 99% of transactions via digital channels. — [Euromoney Awards for Excellence: Mongolia](https://www.euromoney.com/article/2dbsl90eie1o6cyhm1m2o/awards/awards-for-excellence/awards-for-excellence-country-winners-mongolia/)
- Khan Bank 2025: 99.5% of transactions digital, 75% of consumer loans processed digitally; its e-wallet service gained 227,490 users within six months. — [Euromoney 2025 best digital bank](https://www.euromoney.com/article/9nd9g9ebphwc4o4s84ckookg8/awards/awards-for-excellence/awards-for-excellence-country-territory-winners-2025-mongolias-best-digital-bank-khan-bank-jsc/)
- Khan Bank Android app (com.khanbank.retail): 1M+ downloads, rating about 3.9–4.0 from roughly 25.6–25.7K reviews (two snapshots). — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.khanbank.retail)
- Khan Bank App Store description lists account info, transfers, opening current accounts and term deposits, smart notifications, FX rates, branch locator; no spending categorisation/budget feature is mentioned in the listing text seen. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/khan-bank/id1555908766)
- Khan Bank also runs a separate wallet, **DiGi Pay**, promoting fee-free transfers and promotions. — [App Store DiGi Pay](https://apps.apple.com/bb/app/digi-pay/id6478477781)

- SocialPay "has served over 480,000 people" (article date unclear, older than 2024); Golomt has about 1 million customers. — [FinanceAsia](https://www.financeasia.com/article/banking-on-innovation-setting-new-standards-in-mongolia/495311); [Wikipedia: Golomt Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golomt_Bank)

- **M bank**: named Asia-Pacific's Most Innovative Digital Bank by Global Finance (Oct 2026); states an aim to be a "fully digital financial super app" supporting financial well-being. No customer count given. — [Manila Times / press release](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/10/26/tmt-newswire/plentisoft/m-bank-recognized-as-asia-pacifics-most-innovative-digital-bank-by-global-finance-2026/2453508)
- **KakaoBank** named Mongolia as a next market in its global expansion (Apr 2026), a potential new digital-banking entrant/partner. — [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/banking-finance/20260408/kakaobank-targets-mongolia-as-next-market-in-global-expansion)
- **e-Barimt app**: registers receipts by QR, DDTD or lottery number, shows draw dates and winnings; account gives up to 20% VAT refund on registered receipts. — [App Store MN: ebarimt](https://apps.apple.com/mn/app/id1071510270)
- e-Barimt scale: 1,398,018 citizens were due 107.8 billion MNT for the 2026 Q2 VAT refund. — [ikon.mn (Q2 refund transfer)](https://ikon.mn/n/3paa) (URL attribution uncertain among ikon.mn refund articles)
- The April 2026 lottery draw covered 54,794,729 registered receipts for purchases made 1–30 April. — [ikon.mn lottery notices](https://ikon.mn/n/3o5h) (URL attribution uncertain)
- 79,879 citizens paid about 1 billion MNT of their own taxes out of the Q2 2026 refund; 41,020 paid 779.4M MNT out of Q3; 62,613 paid 975.5M out of Q1. — [ikon.mn Q2](https://ikon.mn/n/3p9b); [ikon.mn Q3](https://ikon.mn/n/3rat); [ikon.mn Q1](https://ikon.mn/n/3n3f)
- Receipts for Q3 2026 had to be registered by 23:59:59 on the 8th of the following month, i.e. there is a recurring quarterly deadline users can miss. — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/3qty)
- An older article reported the e-Barimt app at 1.4 million users. — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/20ej) (old; URL attribution uncertain)

### Inferences
- e-Barimt is the single richest itemised purchase dataset on Mongolian consumers (about 55M receipts in one month) and already has about 1.4M engaged citizens, but it is a tax tool with no budgeting layer. A PFM app that ingests e-Barimt data would be differentiated; feasibility is the open question (see section 3).
- Bank apps are per-bank silos. A multi-bank household (Khan salary account + Golomt/SocialPay + MonPay/Toki + 2–3 BNPL apps) has no single view; that aggregation gap is the core opening.
- M bank's "financial well-being super app" positioning makes it the most likely incumbent to ship real PFM features; it is also a plausible partner.

### Gaps
- Whether Khan Bank, Golomt/SocialPay, TDB Online, XacBank, State Bank or M bank apps have spending analytics, category breakdowns, budgets or savings goals: **not found** in any search result. Must be checked hands-on in the apps.
- User counts for SocialPay (current), TDB, XacBank, State Bank, M bank, QPay wallet, Hipay, Pocket, LendMN, Ard App, Numur: not found.
- Store ratings and review themes for SocialPay, MonPay, Toki, Storepay, Pocket, LendMN, e-Barimt: not found (store pages not fetchable).
- Khan Bank's 2025/2026 digital-user total: not found (its 2025 Sustainability Report exists at khanbank.com but could not be opened).

## 3. Can a third-party app get transaction data (open banking, statement exports, SMS/push, e-Barimt API)?

### Takeaway
There is no open-banking regulation or standard in Mongolia as far as could be found, so bank-API aggregation is not available to a startup. The practical routes are user-supplied statement files (Khan Bank exports PDF/TXT/XLS; Golomt exports PDF/Excel and can email statements) and, unverified, notification/SMS parsing. The e-Barimt 3.0 API is merchant/POS-side; no consumer-data API for third parties was evidenced.

### Cited Findings
- Mongolia "does not currently have a formal open banking or open finance regulatory framework". — [Fiskil open finance tracker](https://www.fiskil.com/open-finance-tracker)
- Golomt Bank began researching open banking in 2019 and piloted the following year; the Mongolian Fintech Association notes there are no technical or legal standards to follow when building an open-banking system. — [Mongolian Fintech Association](https://www.mongoliafintech.org/42/nitem)
- Bank directory pages state direct API availability at Mongolian banks (e.g. Khan Bank) "may be limited"; these are generic template pages of low authority. — [Open Banking Tracker: Khan Bank](https://www.openbankingtracker.com/provider/khan-bank-of-mongolia); [country list](https://www.openbankingtracker.com/providers/country/mn)
- A trade-press overview says open banking, instant payments and AI services "accelerated through 2026" in Mongolia but gives no regulatory specifics. — [The Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/fintech-landscape-of-mongolia-in-2026/)
- The FRC hosts a "FinTech Innovation Roadmap" developed Oct 2023–Mar 2025; its contents on open banking could not be read. — [frc.mn PDF](https://www.frc.mn/resources/Image/Document/202602/PPDxs/250328-FinTech-Innovation-Roadmap-(Final)-(1).pdf)
- The Bank of Mongolia prepared a national payment system development strategy for 2022–2026 (payment infrastructure; snippet does not mention data-sharing APIs). — [mongolbank.mn](https://www.mongolbank.mn/file/0e3a41c787a70ce57230a9db429a5545/files/20220909_02.pdf)
- **Khan Bank statement export**: account statement available in PDF, TXT and XLS formats; internet bank lets the user pick account and start/end dates; account reference letters can be sent to an email from the app. — [Khan Bank internet-bank guide](https://www.khanbank.com/uploads/users/28-khanbank/iNTERNET%20BANK%20GUIDENCE.pdf); [Khan Bank app reference guide](https://www.khanbank.com/uploaded/static_files/documents/app%20todorhoilolt.pdf)
- **Golomt statement export**: internet bank "Дансны хуулга" menu, choose period, download as PDF or EXCEL; statement can also be received at an email address. — [Golomt statement guide](https://www.golomtbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/%D0%A5%D1%83%D1%83%D0%BB%D0%B3%D0%B0-%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%85-%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%80.pdf); [Golomt e-bank manual](https://www.golomtbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/EBank-gariin-avlaga.pdf)
- Golomt also offered blockchain-verified statements/reference letters from internet banking (article about 3.7 years old). — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/2qcd)
- **e-Barimt 3.0 (PosAPI 3.0)**: merchant-side API; a B2C receipt issued with the buyer's e-Barimt number as `consumerNo` goes straight to that buyer's account ("easy": true). — [GitHub orshih6/ebarimtv3](https://github.com/orshih6/ebarimtv3)
- Community SDKs list "easy-register" endpoints on service.itc.gov.mn (consumer info lookup, getProfile, approveQr); official reference is the ITC developer portal. — [Go package techpartners-asia/ebarimt-pos3-go](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/techpartners-asia/ebarimt-pos3-go/pos3); [Python SDK README](https://github.com/Amraa1/ebarimt-pos-sdk)
- Credit data: fintech lenders score against the Bank of Mongolia's centralised loan database, i.e. loan data is centralised but accessed by licensed lenders, not consumer apps. — [App Store Pocket.mn](https://apps.apple.com/mn/app/pocket-mn/id1486845825)

### Inferences
- Realistic ingestion for an MVP: (a) upload/share of bank statement XLS/PDF (Khan and Golomt confirmed), (b) forwarding emailed statements to an app-specific inbox (Golomt confirmed; Khan unconfirmed), (c) manual + quick entry. A statement parser per bank is the key build item; Khan Bank's XLS/TXT export is the highest-value first target given its reach.
- The e-Barimt easy-register endpoints appear designed for merchants to identify a buyer, not for a third party to pull a citizen's receipt history; a PFM app would likely need user-driven import (e.g. user exports/screenshots, or scanning each receipt QR in-app) unless the tax authority grants access.
- Because no standard exists, any bank partnership would be bilateral; this is a moat for whoever signs first but slow.

### Gaps
- No Bank of Mongolia open-banking/open-API regulation, draft law, or timeline was found in Mongolian or English. Absence in search is not proof of absence; mongolbank.mn and the FRC roadmap PDF need to be read directly.
- Whether Android notification-listener / SMS parsing of bank alerts is feasible (formats of Khan/Golomt/TDB push and SMS alerts, and whether banks still send SMS vs in-app push): no source found. iOS does not allow reading other apps' notifications or SMS, which limits this to Android (general platform fact, not Mongolia-sourced).
- Statement export formats for TDB, XacBank, State Bank, M bank, MonPay, Toki, SocialPay wallet, Storepay, Pocket, LendMN: not found.
- Whether ebarimt.mn lets a citizen export their own receipt list (Excel/CSV), and what data the consumer lookup returns: not found.

## 4. Market facts: devices, digital adoption, debt burden, financial literacy, willingness to pay

### Takeaway
Mongolia is a small (about 3.5M people), highly digital, heavily indebted consumer market: iOS has an unusually high about 40–45% mobile share, app-based borrowing reaches a reported 1.2–1.7M borrowers, the central bank cut the consumer-loan debt-to-income cap to 45% in January 2026, and measured financial literacy is about half of the maximum score. Evidence of paid consumer subscriptions is limited to content/exam-prep apps at roughly 15k–35k MNT per month.

### Cited Findings
- Mobile OS share (StatCounter, mobile only): Oct 2026 Android 58.69% / iOS 41.29%; June 2026 Android 54.82% / iOS 45.17%; May 2026 Android 60.96% / iOS 39.03%. — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/mongolia)
- 4.97 million cellular mobile connections at end-2025; 2.70 million active social media user identities in Oct 2025. — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Mongolia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mongolia)
- A "Digital 2027: Mongolia" page reports 3.04 million internet users at end-2026 (85.1% penetration) and 2.60 million social media identities in Oct 2026. — [DataReportal Digital 2027: Mongolia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-mongolia); note the social figure is lower than the 2026 report's 2.70M, so the two pages do not line up and should be re-checked.
- Over 80% smartphone ownership (estimate by a payments directory, not a primary statistic). — [PayAtlas](https://payatlas.com/countries/mongolia-mn)
- Facebook reported at 2.1M users in Mongolia (62%), per a local-marketing blog (secondary source). — [datalatte.pro](https://datalatte.pro/blog/local-marketing-mongolia-small-business-2026)
- Debt-to-income cap: Bank of Mongolia set the maximum DTI for new/modified bank consumer loans at 45% (decision of 16 Jan 2026); it had been lowered to 55% in 2024 and then 50%. — [mining.mn](https://mining.mn/content/2334); [assa.mn](https://www.assa.mn/a/72253); [mongolbank.mn MPC statement 2026-01](https://www.mongolbank.mn/mn/r/11422)
- NBFI consumer-loan DTI was set at 60% by the FRC (date and current status unclear). — [gogo.mn explainer](https://gogo.mn/r/e046k9)
- Consumer loan balance reported at 13 trillion MNT, up 2.2x and growing faster than incomes. — [news.mn](https://news.mn/r/2844741/) (URL attribution uncertain; period not stated in snippet)
- Citizens report that tighter bank lending pushes them to higher-interest NBFI loans. — [eguur.mn](https://eguur.mn/577771/)
- App-based (fintech) loans: one source says 1.7 million borrowers have app loans; an ikon.mn opinion piece says 1.2 million. 40 lending apps were registered with the FRC as of a Q3 (year unclear); another source says 59 NBFIs offer fintech loan products. — [ikon.mn opinion](https://ikon.mn/opinion/321w); [news.mn](https://news.mn/r/2754188/) (figures conflict; dates unclear)
- Financial literacy: a Bank of Mongolia-commissioned survey of 1,947 people found an average financial-literacy score of 9.9 out of 20 (49.5%); knowledge sub-score 4.7 of 9; 67.4% understood inflation, 12.1% understood GDP. — [Lemon Press](https://lemonpress.mn/post/fJvgs611BzH); primary report: [mongolbank.mn survey report](https://www.mongolbank.mn/file/f69902beaf64abd22eabe77655140014/files/Mongolbank-survey-report-V3_0-MNG%20(2)(1).pdf)
- Benchmark cited in the same context: the OECD 2020 international survey average for financial knowledge across 26 countries was 62.9%. — [mongolbank.mn survey report](https://www.mongolbank.mn/file/f69902beaf64abd22eabe77655140014/files/Mongolbank-survey-report-V3_0-MNG%20(2)(1).pdf)
- Older baseline (World Bank 2012): 30% did not understand bank interest; over 60% did not know how inflation affects savings. — [World Bank survey deck](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/Worldbank/document/FCCP-survey-ppt-mn.pdf)
- A national programme to raise public financial literacy has run since 2016; in 2025 the Bank of Mongolia launched the "Level-Up" financial education programme (World Money Week, Global Savings Day, school pupils). — [mongolbank.mn Level-Up](https://www.mongolbank.mn/mn/r/8774); [mongolbank.mn 2025 highlights](https://www.mongolbank.mn/mn/r/11222)
- Subscription price points of Mongolian paid apps: M+ (mplus.mn) listed at 14,900₮ per month; YESH.mn exam prep 34,900₮ per month or 199,900₮ per year (pages undated). — [unread.today app roundup](https://unread.today/c/techworm-34mongolapps) (URL attribution uncertain for M+); [yesh.mn](https://yesh.mn/)
- Fintech ecosystem size: Tracxn counted 73 fintech companies (58 active) by Jul 2026, 13 funded, about US$64.6M raised in total. — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/explore/fintech-startups-in-mongolia/__I1YSbgynO7BiDN2aQwUujPSF4sVA2lOV8OkP2SOQwAw)
- Bank of Mongolia published a 2025 cash-usage survey ("Бэлэн мөнгөний хэрэглээний түүвэр судалгаа"); contents could not be read. — [mongolbank.mn](https://www.mongolbank.mn/file/75875dfb00f4dc874a51eaeb5c551a10/files/cash_report_2025_public.pdf)

### Inferences
- With iOS at about 40–45%, an Android-only launch would forfeit close to half the market and probably the higher-income half; iOS must be in v1. It also means Android-only data tricks (notification parsing) cover only about 55–60% of users.
- Total addressable base is roughly the 1.4M e-Barimt refund recipients to 1.8M+ Khan digital users; the debt-stressed segment (1.2–1.7M app borrowers) is the most acute-need subgroup.
- Tightening DTI caps plus widespread multi-app borrowing make "how much do I owe, to whom, due when" a sharper pain than classic budgeting.
- Observed paid price points (about 15k–35k MNT per month for content/education) are an upper bound for a utility app; a PFM subscription would more plausibly sit below that. This is an estimate, not a measured willingness-to-pay.

### Gaps

## 5. How do Mongolian consumer apps acquire users, and what does it cost?

### Takeaway
Hard data on acquisition tactics and costs was largely unobtainable. What is evidenced: Facebook dominates reach, paid media is cheap and under-used, banks drive usage with cashback promotions, the state drives e-Barimt engagement with a monthly lottery and quarterly refunds, and BNPL apps spread through merchant checkout co-marketing.

### Cited Findings
- Most Mongolian businesses rely on organic Facebook; paid search is largely untapped; competition for ads is minimal even in Ulaanbaatar; Google Ads CPC roughly US$0.15–1.18 depending on industry (secondary blog source). — [datalatte.pro](https://datalatte.pro/blog/local-marketing-mongolia-small-business-2026)
- Country CPM tables put Mongolia at very low rates (about US$0.06–0.20 per 1,000 impressions); these come from low-authority list sites and conflict with each other. — [worldpopulationreview CPM by country](https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/cpm-rates-by-country); [thesrzone](https://www.thesrzone.com/2024/03/facebook-cpm-rates-by-country.html)
- Bank cashback as an acquisition/activation lever: Khan Bank 10% cashback for credit-card users at selected merchants (Aug–Sep 2026). — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/3pik)
- Lottery as an engagement mechanic: VAT lottery draws are held monthly and broadcast live in the e-Barimt app and on TV; a named receipt number winning 5–30 million MNT is publicised in the press. — [ikon.mn draw notice](https://ikon.mn/n/3r1o); [ikon.mn winner story](https://ikon.mn/n/3nmw)
- Merchant-side distribution: retailers announce "now payable with Storepay / Pocket Zero / Lend Pay" on their own channels, giving BNPL apps free reach at the point of purchase. — [goldenbeauty.mn](https://goldenbeauty.mn/posts/27922); [beautylock.mn](https://beautylock.mn/posts/35703)
- Sponsored-content placement on ikon.mn is a common launch channel (bank and retailer promotions appear as news items there). — [ikon.mn Nomin promotion](https://ikon.mn/n/34ln)
- App roundups by unread.today ("34 useful Mongolian apps", "Useful Mongolian apps #2") are an earned-media channel for local apps. — [unread.today](https://unread.today/c/techworm-34mongolapps); [unread.today #2](https://unread.today/c/1265)

### Inferences
- A lottery/refund hook is culturally proven (about 55M receipts registered per month); tying budgeting to "never miss the e-Barimt deadline, see your expected refund" borrows an existing habit.
- Cheap Facebook/Instagram inventory plus low ad competition suggests paid install campaigns are affordable, but no Mongolia-specific cost-per-install figure exists to plan against; it must be measured with a small test budget.

## 6. What payment options exist for charging subscriptions (QPay, Apple/Google IAP)?

### Takeaway
Both rails are usable: Google Play supports developer and merchant registration in Mongolia (paid apps and IAP), and QPay offers a merchant API at a 1% fee with a one-time 300,000 MNT fee for online deep-link integration. Apple-side specifics for Mongolia were not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Google's supported-locations table lists Mongolia as supporting both Google Play developer registration and merchant registration; default developer currency USD. Buyers must also be in a supported location. — [Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9306917?hl=en-GB)
- Mongolia was among countries added for Play developer registration by 2019. — [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/2019/07/02/google-play-developer-and-merchant-registration-come-to-palestine-somalia-and-many-more-countries/)
- QPay merchant service fee is 1% of the transaction amount; government bodies exempt; no extra equipment or connection cost listed for standard use. — [QPay developer docs](https://developer.qpay.mn/)
- The 1% is split among payer's bank, merchant's bank, Bank of Mongolia and QPay; no fee for the paying customer when scanning a QPay QR. — [QPay static QR page](https://qr.qpay.mn/static-qr)
- Online-platform (Deep Link) integration has a one-time connection fee of 300,000 MNT. — [QPay Deep Link page](https://qr.qpay.mn/deep-link)
- Onboarding: contract signed online at business.qpay.mn; API integration requires obtaining OAuth 2.0 client credentials from QPay. — [QPay developer docs](https://developer.qpay.mn/)
- QPay describes itself as connecting all Mongolian commercial banks and digital wallets. — [qpay.mn](https://qpay.mn/)
- Local apps selling access outside the stores use fixed-term passes rather than auto-renewal (e.g. T-Test access valid 4 months from payment, then re-verify). — [t-test.mn](https://t-test.mn/)

### Inferences
- QPay (1%) is far cheaper than store IAP commissions (standard 15–30%, general platform fact), but QPay payments are one-off pushes, so "subscription" in practice means prepaid 1/3/12-month passes, as local apps do. Apple/Google rules on selling digital features outside IAP apply and need a compliance check.
- Offering prepaid passes via QPay plus store IAP for convenience is the likely dual-rail setup.

### Gaps
- Apple App Store: whether Mongolian developers can receive payouts locally, MNT price tiers, and Mongolian card acceptance for IAP: not found.
- Whether QPay supports recurring/auto-debit billing; QPay consumer wallet user count; SocialPay/MonPay/Toki merchant terms: not found.
- Share of Mongolian users with a card attached to Apple ID / Google Play: not found.

## 7. What unmet needs exist?

### Takeaway
No source directly documents unmet PFM needs in Mongolia; the gaps below are inferred from what exists and what does not. The strongest evidence-backed gaps are cross-lender loan/instalment visibility and an e-Barimt refund/lottery companion; family budgeting and micro-business bookkeeping are addressed only by tiny local apps.

### Cited Findings
- Multi-lender instalment exposure is normal: merchants offer Storepay, Pocket Zero and Lend Pay together. — [goldenbeauty.mn](https://goldenbeauty.mn/posts/27922)
- Reported 1.2–1.7 million app-loan borrowers; opinion press describes people borrowing to roll over app loans. — [ikon.mn opinion "Аппликэйшний зээлээ сунгах гэсэн юм. Мөнгө зээлээч?"](https://ikon.mn/opinion/321w)
- Press covers the path "from loan pressure to the bad-borrower list". — [news.mn](https://news.mn/r/2754188/)
- Regulators are tightening consumer credit (bank DTI cap 45% from Jan 2026). — [mining.mn](https://mining.mn/content/2334)
- The only loan-tracking tool found is the Bank of Mongolia website calculator where the user types in loan details manually. — [sankhuugiinbolovsrol.mn/Tools](https://www.sankhuugiinbolovsrol.mn/Tools)
- e-Barimt has hard quarterly registration deadlines and about 1.4M refund recipients; tens of thousands choose to offset personal taxes from the refund each quarter. — [ikon.mn deadline](https://ikon.mn/n/3qty); [ikon.mn Q2 tax offset](https://ikon.mn/n/3p9b)
- Family/household mode exists only in small apps (MyFin "household and personal in parallel"; Finexy "family mode"). — [MyFin App Store](https://apps.apple.com/qa/app/myfin-%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE-%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B3%D0%B0/id1563540663); [Finexy App Store](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/finexy/id6759546900)
- Small-business use is a stated secondary audience of MyFin; separately, developers are building e-Barimt 3.0 POS/accounting integrations for businesses. — [MyFin App Store](https://apps.apple.com/qa/app/myfin-%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE-%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B3%D0%B0/id1563540663); [GitHub entry-accounting PR](https://github.com/Tuguldur0107/entry-accounting/pull/51)
- Measured financial literacy is 9.9/20, and the central bank is actively running education programmes, i.e. institutional appetite for tools that improve money management. — [Lemon Press](https://lemonpress.mn/post/fJvgs611BzH); [mongolbank.mn Level-Up](https://www.mongolbank.mn/mn/r/8774)

### Inferences
- **Loan/instalment dashboard across Storepay, Pocket, LendMN and bank loans** (due dates, total monthly obligation vs salary, DTI-style gauge): no product found; highest-pain gap given borrower counts and DTI tightening.
- **Salary-to-salary planning**: nothing found locally; fits a market with heavy consumer credit and (anecdotally) loan rollover behaviour.
- **e-Barimt companion** (deadline reminders, expected refund estimate, lottery tracking, receipts as expense entries): no third-party product found; rides an existing mass habit.
- **Statement-import auto-categorisation in Mongolian** for Khan/Golomt files: no local app found doing it; removes the manual-entry burden that limits the existing trackers.
- **Family budgets** and **individual-entrepreneur bookkeeping** are claimed by MyFin/Finexy but with no evidence of traction; they are differentiators only if executed markedly better.
- Partnership angle: the Bank of Mongolia / FRC financial-literacy programme and M bank's "financial well-being" positioning are natural distribution allies.

### Gaps
- No user research, survey, or review corpus was found that states these needs in consumers' own words; all of section 7's needs are inferences and should be validated with interviews.
- App-store review complaints for bank/wallet apps (which would reveal feature gaps) could not be retrieved.
- Number of registered individual entrepreneurs / micro-businesses and their current bookkeeping tools: not researched to a sourced figure.
